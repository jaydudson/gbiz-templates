# Chase Nudge Skill

## When to use
When producing or updating the draft follow-up list for quiet quotes or enquiries.

## Required inputs and access
The client’s connected email tool (whatever they use). Agreed silence threshold (default 5 business days). Any prior approved draft examples for voice.

## Sequence of work
1. Search the client’s email for open quotes, proposals, or unanswered enquiries older than the threshold.
2. For each candidate, extract name, days silent, and relevant context only.
3. Draft a short polite subject + body (≤80 words) in the client’s voice. No invented details.
4. Present the full list using the exact deliverable schema.
5. Stop and wait for approval. Do not send.

## How to validate
Every item has name, days silent, subject, and body. No invented data. List is draft-only. Output matches schema.

## What to return
The draft nudge list in the required format. Note any items skipped and why.

## What requires approval
Sending any message. Changing the silence threshold. Expanding the job beyond drafting nudges.
